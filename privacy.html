Site Sums

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Site Sums ("the app") is made by Christian Augustuss Bergara ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: we don't collect any of your data. There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The app stores the measurements and settings you enter and, if you have Pro, the jobs, rooms and shopping lists you save. All of it is kept only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we can't see your data. It's included in your own iPhone backup if you have backups turned on; that backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, or any third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't use your location, camera, photos or contacts.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your device. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Sharing

When you share a job summary (PDF or text), it goes only where you choose to send it, using your iPhone's share sheet. We don't receive a copy.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it goes from your own mail app to lixcab@icloud.com. We receive what you write, your email address and, only if you turn the option on, the app version and your iPhone model. We use this only to reply to you and improve the app. We keep feedback emails for up to 24 months and then delete them. Ask us at any time to delete them sooner.

Deleting your data

You can delete calculations and jobs in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you can ask us for a copy of, or the deletion of, any feedback emails you've sent us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions: lixcab@icloud.com
