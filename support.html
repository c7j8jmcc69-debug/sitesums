Support

Questions, bugs or calculator ideas? Email lixcab@icloud.com, or use Settings › Send feedback in the app. We usually reply within two working days.

Is it free?

Yes. The paint and tile calculators are free, with full results, and always will be. Pro is a single payment with no subscription. It adds saved jobs, a combined shopping list, PDF and text sharing, imperial units and every future calculator.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Site Sums and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Pro also works for your Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Everything works offline, including Pro once it's unlocked.

How accurate are the results?

They're estimates based on standard formulas and the figures you enter. Use the coverage figure printed on your tin, and check against the product label and your project requirements. Every result lists the assumptions it used.

Why does it suggest 7.5 L when I need 6.55 L?

It suggests tins you can actually buy (10 L, 5 L and 2.5 L) that cover what you need with the least left over. It also shows the litres rounded up, so you can see both.

How are tiles worked out?

The waste for your layout is added to the area first. That's divided by the area of one tile and rounded up to whole tiles, then any spares you ask for are added. Boxes are rounded up from that total.

Where is my data?

Only on your iPhone, and in your normal iPhone backup. Deleting the app removes all of its data. See the privacy policy.
